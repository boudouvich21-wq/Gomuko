GoMoKu

How to play

  1. Two players are matched automatically: Black moves first, White moves second.
  2. Click one empty square on your turn to place your stone on both boards.
  3. Make five stones in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  4. Invalid moves, including playing twice in a row, are rejected by the server.
